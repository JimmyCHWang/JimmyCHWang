## What I build

I enjoy turning intricate rules and workflows into tools people can actually use—from interactive editors and livestream controls to mahjong calculators and puzzle-hunt websites.

- **Mahjong tools:** I build and maintain scoring, training, and tournament tools for Mahjong Competition Rules and Riichi Mahjong. [MCR Helper](https://mcr-helper.jimmychwang.com/) supports hand calculation and in-person game records; my [mahjong tools collection](https://mahjong.jimmychwang.com/) brings the projects together. I also contribute to the ScoreMJ community scoring platform, including its broadcast display and control tools.
- **Puzzle hunts and community archives:** I contribute to [PKU Puzzle Club's information site](https://github.com/PKUPC/pkupc-info), including event archives and puzzle articles, and work on puzzle-hunt websites and tools. I'm also a member of **T34 Studio**, where I worked on *Ning's Wing 3: The Hexad Trials*.
- **Product engineering:** My professional work includes cross-platform LIVE commerce experiences, agency management applications, and interactive editors. [Selected work →](https://portfolio.jimmychwang.com/en/work/)
